Lisbon, the first of March, 1451

Prince Henry's captains have pushed past Cape Bojador. You own one caravel, the São Cristóvão, and a purse of 300 cruzados. Everything south of the known coast is uncharted.

  • Click the sea to set a course. Click a port to sail there. Shift-click adds a waypoint.
  • Mind the wind. The north-east trades carry you south fast. Beating home against them is slow; swinging out to sea and catching the westerlies is often quicker.
  • Buy low, sell high. Trading moves prices, and they recover over weeks.
  • Your price book only knows prices you have seen, and they go stale.
  • Make a name for yourself. Once your purse reaches 500 cruzados, the crown and the merchant houses will start offering you contracts.
  • The crown pays for new charts when you return to Lisbon.
